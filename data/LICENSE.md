# Data sources

The files in this folder are built from open data by the scripts in `tools/`, and keep their sources' licenses.

| File | Source | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `strokes.json` | [KanjiVG](https://kanjivg.tagaini.net), copyright © 2009–2026 Ulrich Apel, built by `tools/build-strokes.js` | [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) |
| `vocab.json` | The JLPT N5 and N4 lists of [open-anki-jlpt-decks](https://github.com/jamsinclair/open-anki-jlpt-decks) (MIT, © 2020 Jamie Sinclair, after tanos.co.uk); word types and frequency from [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), the property of the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group, used in conformance with the Group's [licence](https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html); kanji levels (the pre-2010 JLPT levels) from [KANJIDIC2](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/KANJIDIC_Project), also the Group's property and used under the same licence; example sentences from [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org) (CC BY 2.0 FR), and for 80 words Tatoeba has none for, written here in `tools/examples.js`. Built by `tools/build-vocab.js` | [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) |
| `grammar.json` | Grammar explanations adapted and shortened from Wikibooks' [JLPT Guide/JLPT N5 Grammar](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/JLPT_Guide/JLPT_N5_Grammar) (CC BY-SA 4.0); sentences from [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org) (CC BY 2.0 FR), each kept with its Tatoeba id, and 8 written here; word readings from `vocab.json` and kanji levels from [KANJIDIC2](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/KANJIDIC_Project) as for `vocab.json`. Built by `tools/build-grammar.js` from `tools/grammar-points.js` | [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) |
| `kanji.json` | Meanings, stroke counts and pre-2010 JLPT levels from [KANJIDIC2](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/KANJIDIC_Project), the property of the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group, used in conformance with the Group's [licence](https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html), for every kanji in `vocab.json`; named components from [KanjiVG](https://kanjivg.tagaini.net), copyright © 2009–2026 Ulrich Apel (CC BY-SA 3.0, which allows a later version of the licence); each kanji's main reading (`top`) from the anime word frequency list of [Jiten](https://jiten.moe/frequency-dictionaries) (CC BY-SA 4.0), downloaded to `_data/jiten/anime.csv`. Built by `tools/build-kanji.js` | [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) |
| `history.json` | Notes and stories on where a kanji's shape comes from, written here in our own words from the "Glyph origin" sections of [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org) (CC BY-SA 4.0), fetched by `tools/fetch-wiktionary.js`; where an origin is disputed, the traditional explanation is given as such | [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) |
| `kanji-strokes.json` | [KanjiVG](https://kanjivg.tagaini.net), copyright © 2009–2026 Ulrich Apel, strokes for every kanji in `vocab.json`, built by `tools/build-kanji.js` | [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) |
| `words/` | Word recordings from [tofugu/japanese-vocabulary-pronunciation-audio](https://github.com/tofugu/japanese-vocabulary-pronunciation-audio), old [WaniKani](https://www.wanikani.com) audio by [Tofugu](https://www.tofugu.com) and WaniKani, renamed by word key and fetched by `tools/fetch-wk-audio.js` | [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) |
